Breast Cancer Awareness Session

October 28, 2025

In line with this year’s theme — “Every Story is Unique, Every Journey Matters” — Sante held a session to spread awareness, encourage early detection and honor the strength of every journey. The session was conducted by Dr. Fatima, a leading oncologist, who shared valuable insights on prevention, self-examination, and the latest advancements in treatment.

The event was attended by employees from all departments, who actively participated in the Q&A session. Sante is committed to the health and well-being of its employees and will continue to organize such informative events in the future.
